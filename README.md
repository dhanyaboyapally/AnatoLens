# 🫀 AnatoLens

### **Don’t just study anatomy. Step inside it.**

AnatoLens is an **AI-powered interactive 3D anatomy learning platform** that transforms traditional anatomy study into an immersive, hands-on experience.

Instead of memorizing structures from static textbook diagrams, students can **explore the human body in 3D, dissect anatomical layers, interact with individual structures, take notes, test their knowledge, and learn through clinical scenarios — all in one platform.**

---

## 💡 Inspiration

Learning anatomy often means switching between textbooks, diagrams, videos, flashcards, and separate 3D applications.

We wanted to create something different.

**What if students could actually explore anatomy instead of simply memorizing it?**

That idea became **AnatoLens** — an interactive environment where learners can visualize anatomical relationships, investigate structures, and connect anatomy to real clinical situations.

---

## 🚀 What AnatoLens Does

AnatoLens provides an interactive 3D human anatomy environment designed around active learning.

### 🧍 Interactive 3D Anatomy

Explore the human body using an interactive 3D model.

Users can:

- Rotate and zoom around the body
- Select anatomical structures
- Focus the camera on specific structures
- Hide or isolate structures
- Explore relationships between different anatomical systems

---

### 🔪 Virtual Dissection

Move beyond surface-level visualization.

Dissection Mode allows learners to progressively remove anatomical structures and explore deeper layers of the body.

This helps students understand **where structures are located and how they relate spatially to surrounding anatomy.**

---

### 🧬 Tissue & Anatomy Layers

Explore anatomy by system and layer, including structures such as:

- Skeletal system
- Muscular system
- Nervous system
- Vascular structures
- Organs and deeper anatomical structures

Users can control layer visibility to focus on exactly what they are studying.

---

### 🤖 AI Anatomy Assistant

AnatoLens includes an integrated AI learning assistant.

Students can ask anatomy questions while exploring the model and receive explanations without leaving the learning environment.

The assistant is designed to help learners:

- Understand anatomical structures
- Review functions
- Clarify difficult concepts
- Navigate to relevant anatomy
- Connect structures with clinical concepts

---

### 📌 Interactive Notes

Students can create notes while studying anatomical structures.

Instead of keeping learning materials separate from the model, AnatoLens allows students to associate their observations directly with their anatomy exploration.

---

### 🧠 Revision Mode

Revision Mode turns exploration into active recall.

Students can review anatomical structures and test whether they can identify them without relying on labels.

This encourages **active learning rather than passive memorization.**

---

### 🩺 Clinical Challenge Mode

Anatomy becomes much more meaningful when connected to medicine.

Clinical Challenge Mode presents real-world inspired scenarios where students must use anatomical knowledge to understand symptoms and identify relevant structures.

For example, a learner may investigate a **wrist drop** scenario and explore the anatomical structures associated with the condition.

This connects:

**Anatomy → Function → Symptoms → Clinical reasoning**

---

### ❓ Knowledge Checks & Quizzes

AnatoLens includes interactive quizzes that allow students to test their understanding while exploring the model.

This provides immediate reinforcement and makes studying more engaging.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Traditional anatomy education often relies heavily on:

- Static textbook diagrams
- Memorization
- Separate learning resources
- Limited spatial visualization
- Few opportunities to connect anatomy with clinical reasoning

Understanding complex three-dimensional relationships from two-dimensional images can be difficult.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

AnatoLens combines:

**3D visualization + virtual dissection + AI assistance + active recall + clinical learning**

into one interactive platform.

Instead of asking students to simply **remember where something is**, AnatoLens encourages them to **find it, explore it, understand it, and apply it.**
